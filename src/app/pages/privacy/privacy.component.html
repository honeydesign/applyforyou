Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Apply-4You ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Apply-4You website and Chrome extension. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use our service:

  • Name and email address when you create an account
  • CV/resume text that you upload or paste
  • Job preferences you set (job title, location, work type)
  • Job application data (which jobs were applied to, ATS scores)

Chrome Extension

Our Chrome extension collects:

  • Your Apply-4You login token (stored locally on your device)
  • Job listing data from LinkedIn and Indeed pages you visit while using the extension
  • Application submission data synced to your Apply-4You account

The extension does NOT collect your LinkedIn or Indeed passwords. It uses your existing browser session to submit applications.

How We Use Your Information

  • To tailor your CV using AI for each job application
  • To submit job applications on your behalf
  • To track your application history on your dashboard
  • To send you email notifications about your applications

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Railway (PostgreSQL database). We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Third Party Services

  • Anthropic Claude API — used to tailor CVs and generate cover letters
  • Resend — used to send email notifications
  • Railway — used to host our backend and database
  • Vercel — used to host our frontend

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data at any time. You can do this from your account Settings page or by contacting us.
